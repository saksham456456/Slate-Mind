🛡️ Trust, Privacy & Legal Principles

SlateMind is designed as a local-first software tool, not an extractive platform.

1. Local-First & Data Minimization

All your learning profile data, XP, streaks, levels, and lesson history live entirely in your browser's local storage. We do not require accounts, logins, passwords, emails, or personal tracking.

2. Transparent AI Processing

When you request a lesson or follow-up question, the topic query is sent server-side to the Groq API solely to generate educational blackboard JSON. Prompts are processed transiently and are never used to train private user profiles.

3. Minors & Safe Learning

SlateMind contains keyword filtering and safety guardrails against inappropriate topics. Educational tools used by minors should be utilized with guardian awareness.

4. Pricing & No Hidden Fees

SlateMind is a free educational tool. Any future premium tiers for high-resolution video exports or classroom deployments follow honest, one-time or upfront pricing with no dark patterns, automatic subscriptions, or deceptive countdowns.

5. Data Deletion (Right to Be Forgotten)

You can completely wipe all locally saved data at any time with the "Clear My Data" button located in the Settings panel.
